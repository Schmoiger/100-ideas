## Core Thesis & Synopsis

Because modern enterprises run on software, the software development lifecycle forms the critical path for business expansion. Engineering capacity cannot keep pace with business demand when bound by manual developer bandwidth. Autonomous agents remove this throughput limit, turning software delivery from a capacity bottleneck into a scalable growth engine.

## Economic Trade-offs & Constraint Dynamics

- Bottleneck migration: When adopting 'Software Development as the Binding Enterprise Growth Constraint', engineering capacity expands upstream, shifting operational friction to review queues, automated test execution, and deployment pipelines.
- Opportunity cost: Allocating engineering bandwidth to manual inspection rather than governing automated verification guard rails creates a high learning tax and slows cycle times.

## Counterarguments, Tensions & Anti-Patterns
